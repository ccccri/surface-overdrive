# Features

What the previous project (`surface-go-kinoite`) did, and where each piece stands here. Anything about the keyboard cover (Type Cover) is out of scope: the tablet is used as a tablet.

| # | Feature | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Patched kernel modules: cameras, NFC, volume buttons | **done**, built in CI-style stage, verified on the tablet |
| 2 | Patched libcamera (crash fixes, autofocus, exposure, black level per sensor mode, flicker, start-up behaviour) | **done** |
| 3 | NFC reader daemon (`overdrive-nfcd`) | **done**, running from the image; reading a real tag still to be tried |
| 4 | NFC notifications (`overdrive-nfc-notify`) | **done** |
| 5 | Volume buttons: hold to repeat, with an on/off switch (`overdrivectl volume-hold`) | **done** |
| 6 | Pen: hide the fake 0% battery (HID-BPF), with a switch (`overdrivectl stylus-filter`) | **done** |
| 7 | Speakers: louder (software gain on a filter-chain sink, default sink) | **done**; the equaliser is not ported yet |
| 8 | Microphone: sane hardware level (+18 dB, boost off) | **done**; noise suppression and the enhancer are not ported yet |
| 9 | Plasma volume: up to 150%, 5% steps | **done** |
| 10 | Plymouth scale 1, GRUB 3 s countdown | **done**: boot-time service `overdrive-bootconfig` keeps a marked block in `/boot/grub2/user.cfg` |
| 11 | Sensors: udev rule lets the settings page raise the accelerometer/gyro rate | **done** (`61-overdrive-sensors.rules`) |
| 12 | Health monitor with explanations (`overdrivectl status`) and a desktop notification when a fix stops working (user timer `overdrive-health-watch`) | **done**; repair actions and the update guard are not yet |
| 13 | Our pages in System Settings, written to blend in with Plasma's (same tab bars, section headings, check boxes, scroll bars). Two pages of our own under Input & Output: "Cameras" and "NFC". Five Plasma pages are replaced by ours, which load the Plasma page and add our part to it: "Sound and EQ" (boost and equalizer right under the playback devices, microphone enhancement right under the recording devices), "Drawing Tablet" (Plasma's Display and Pen tabs plus a pen test next to the pen settings; Pad and the unsupported left-handed mode left out), "Power Management" (one line of battery figures at the bottom), "About this System" (firmware in the hardware list, light and motion sensors next to it) and "Software Update" ("Kernel Patches and Modules" first, Plasma's update settings under it) | **done**: C++ KCMs + QML, run `overdrivectl`; the Plasma plugins are moved to `plasma/kcms/overdrive-stock` in the image and loaded from there. Pages a Plasma page opens on top of itself take the whole view, as in Plasma |
| 14 | Cameras page: live preview, live editor, per-camera presets, manual focus, rainbow | **done** (`overdrivectl camera`, page "Cameras"); needs the user to judge the looks |
| 15 | Sound: speaker boost, 10-band equalizer per output (presets, reset, APO/AutoEQ import and export), limiter, left/right swap per output (on for the headphone jack), microphone enhancement (noise suppression, low cut, three bands, gain). All of it runs **inside the built-in devices** (PipeWire filter graphs loaded into the device nodes), so the device lists keep one entry per real device | **done** (`overdrivectl audio`, user service `overdrive-audio-route` loads the filters at login and after a sound server restart; own LADSPA plugin `ladspa/overdrive_denoise.c` on rnnoise). Measured through the speakers with the microphone; headphones not yet tried by the user |
| 16 | NFC page: tags shown in the page (live from the daemon), reader test | **done**; real tag not yet tried by the user |
| 17 | Pen page: pen test (pressure, tilt, buttons), Bluetooth pen battery, filter switch | **done**; the pen itself not yet tried by the user |
| 18 | Sensors (light, accelerometer/gyro bars, firmware) at the end of "About this System"; battery line (charge, power, health, cycles) above Plasma's Power Management page | **done** (2D bars, the old 3D model view was not ported) |
| 19 | Updates and log; export/import of presets | **done** (page "Software Update": the system images with a way back and an undo; "What Changed" lists the changes of this project between the two images, from the commit each image was built from (label `org.surfaceoverdrive.source-sha`, asked from GitHub), and the packages; export and import of all Surface settings (sound, camera profiles and presets) with an undo; a problem report without user, host or addresses). Tried on the tablet 2026-10-28 |
| 20 | Installer wizard | replaced by `bootstrap.sh` (one command, one reboot, MOK enrolment), started with `curl -L ccccri.github.io\|bash`; verified on a fresh Kinoite 44 install |
| 21 | Own colour calibration: black level, lens shading, colour matrix (tools in `tools/calib`, the laptop screen as a light source driven by `screen.py`) | **rear camera done** (2026-10-18: corners within 3% in colour in every stream size, matrix error 0.006); front camera and the white balance under warm lights (a locus between real lights) not yet |
| 22 | CI that rebuilds the image daily | **done**: the nightly image of 2026-10-18 reached the tablet through Discover and booted |
| 23 | Unlock with a PIN or a 9-dot pattern next to the password: lock screen (after idle, after sleep) and login screen (cold start). Chosen in "Screen Locking" (our page around Plasma's), set through polkit after the password; five wrong tries leave the password only until it is used | **done** (`src/overdrive/unlock.py`, PAM line from `ci/patch_pam.py`, pads in `/usr/share/surface-overdrive/unlock`, login screen rebuilt by `ci/build_login.sh`); verified on the tablet from a test install, not yet from a CI image |
| 24 | Apps of the image: Xournal++ and Kamoso added, KMines, Elisa and KolourPaint removed (`overdrive-flatpaks.service`: `flatpak preinstall` plus a remove-once list) | **done**, tried on the tablet |
| 25 | Tablet mode of our own: Plasma 6.7 only enlarges the tray icons in tablet mode, so follow KWin's tablet mode ourselves (larger title bar buttons, taller and wider spaced task bar; back to normal with a keyboard or mouse) | not yet |
| 26 | Pictures in Kamoso: every shot restarts the camera at the picture size, so exposure and focus start over and some pictures come out blurred or dark (and focus feels worse than in our Cameras page) | not yet |
| 27 | One wallpaper for desktop, lock screen and login screen (the desktop's, copied on every change; the login copy through a polkit helper that gets only pixels) | **done** (`overdrive-wallpaper` user service + `login-wallpaper`) |
| 28 | Opinionated KDE settings of the owner, shipped as defaults of the image | not yet (list to come) |
| 29 | Front camera colours and noise | **done** (2026-10-25): colours tuned, chroma noise down with XNR3; the remaining luma grain is the sensor's |
| 30 | Touch in the GRUB menu (the firmware passes touch to boot programs: rEFInd could use it; the volume keys do not reach GRUB) | idea for later: a GRUB build signed with our key, risky for little use |
| 31 | Video recording with the GPU's encoder (VA-API H.264 on the HD 615): RPM Fusion's intel-media-driver in the image (Fedora's has no H.264/HEVC) and our camera app `overdrive-camera` (preview, photos, H.264 + AAC videos with real timestamps) | **done** (2026-10-26). Kamoso stays for whoever wants it, with its software Theora |
| 32 | Rear camera finished: tuned look, clean start, every size, stress-tested (tools/dev/rear_stress.sh: 40 start/stop at every size, 15 size changes, two clients at once, both cameras in turn, 10 minutes at 30.0 fps with steady memory, suspend and resume with the camera streaming) | **done** (2026-10-25). Both cameras at once is not possible: a known limit of libcamera's IPU3 pipeline (the second gets no frames, the first goes on) |
